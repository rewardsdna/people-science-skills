# Organizational Behavior Decisioning Skill

**Builds on:** `people-science-core-reasoning`

Use this skill together with the Core Reasoning skill whenever the decision or analysis involves workplace behavior, culture, leadership, motivation, teams, norms, power, change, or organizational systems.

---

## 🎯 Executive Usability & Decision Mandate

1. **Zero Consulting Boilerplate**: Start responses immediately with the behavioral intervention decision.
2. **Make an Actual Decision (No Premature Interventions)**: Recommend a specific organizational intervention. If organizational culture or leadership evidence is unverified, recommend a **Behavioral Baseline Audit** or **Department Pilot** before company-wide rollout.
3. **The Evidence Gatekeeper Condition**: Every recommendation MUST specify an explicit Gatekeeper Condition: *"Do NOT authorize company-wide rollout of [Intervention X] until [Pilot Outcome Y] is verified."*
4. **No Synthetic Statistics**: Never fabricate percentage figures or fake study citations.

---

## 🔬 Mandatory Required Output Structure

Every response MUST follow this structure:

### 1. 🎯 Executive Summary (BLUF)
- **Recommended OB Intervention**: [Clear 1-sentence decision; Pilot if evidence is incomplete]
- **Behavioral Mechanism**: [Observable human behavior & psychological need addressed]
- **Gatekeeper Condition**: [Explicit pilot metric required before full rollout]

### 2. 📊 Intervention Options & Decision Matrix
| Intervention Option | Primary Behavioral Mechanism | Key Risk & Trade-off | Recommendation Tier |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Option A (Systemic Work Redesign)** | [Mechanism] | [Risk] | ✅ Recommended |
| **Option B (Leadership Capability Training)**| [Mechanism] | [Risk] | ⚠️ Secondary |
| **Option C (Pop HR / Culture Slogan)** | [Mechanism] | [Zero impact] | ❌ Rejected |

### 3. 🔬 Evidence & Causal Discipline Audit
1. **Association vs. Causation**: Audit workplace survey correlations vs. causal experiments.
2. **Confounding & Reverse Causality**: Identify at least two confounders or selection biases.
3. **Evidence Tiers & Gatekeeper**: Grade evidence explicitly (Strongly Supported / Plausible / Assumption / Unknown) and state the pilot verification requirement.

### 4. 📅 30-60-90 Day Operational Roadmap
- **Days 1–30**: Baseline behavioral observation, structural constraint audit, and Gatekeeper verification.
- **Days 31–60**: Pilot intervention on target department/cohort.
- **Days 61–90**: Evaluate behavioral outcome, feedback loops, and organization-wide rollout.
