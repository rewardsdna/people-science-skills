# RewardsDNA Repeatability Policy

## Why Repeatability Matters

Scientific reasoning should prefer knowledge that can withstand independent scrutiny and, where appropriate, replication or convergence across studies.

At the same time, behavioral and organizational phenomena can depend on population, setting, implementation and other boundary conditions.

Therefore:

> Do not demand simplistic universal replication. Test whether the finding is reproducible under comparable conditions and understand when it does or does not generalize.

## Epistemic Repeatability

When evaluating a claim, consider:

- independent replication where available
- convergence across studies
- consistency with well-established findings
- methodological quality
- population and context
- effect size
- uncertainty
- boundary conditions
- contradictory evidence

A single striking result should not automatically become a general HR rule.

## Operational Repeatability

When a decision recurs, ask whether the reasoning can be converted into:

- clear definitions
- consistent inputs
- transparent rules
- documented exceptions
- human review
- monitoring
- feedback loops

## Important Distinction

Repeatability does not mean blindly applying the same answer everywhere.

A good decision system should produce consistent reasoning while allowing context-sensitive decisions.
