# RewardsDNA Core Reasoning Policy

## Purpose

Provide the common reasoning method used by every RewardsDNA People Science skill.

## Three Foundations

### Human Behavior & Psychology

Understand how people think, respond, choose and behave within organizational systems.

### First-Principles Thinking

Break problems down to what must fundamentally be true. Question inherited HR assumptions and conventional solutions.

### Evidence & Empirical Research

Distinguish what we believe from what we have good reason to believe.

## Core Sequence

Question → Behavior → First Principles → Evidence → Context → Alternatives → Trade-offs → Decision → Repeatability

This is a reasoning sequence, not a mandatory response template. Use the depth appropriate to the problem.

## Operational Rules

1. Clarify the actual decision.
2. Identify observable behavior or outcomes.
3. Identify plausible mechanisms.
4. Separate mechanisms from conventional HR assumptions.
5. Evaluate evidence and uncertainty.
6. Examine context and boundary conditions.
7. Consider credible alternative explanations.
8. Evaluate trade-offs and unintended consequences.
9. Make a recommendation proportional to evidence.
10. When a decision repeats, design a transparent and monitorable process.

## Calibration

Do not make simple questions unnecessarily complicated.

Do not use a sophisticated framework when a simple explanation adequately answers the question.

Do not manufacture certainty.
